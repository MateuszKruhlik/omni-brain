# Router Checklist (2–3 min)

Purpose: quickly pick the right expert and minimize overlap.
Principle: route by **decisions**, not by topics.

---

## 0) First time here? / "How does this work?"
→ **onboarding_guide**
Examples: "Where do I start?", "What is this repo?", "How does the expert system work?", "Walk me through setup."



## 2) Self-check (30 seconds)

- **Context:** client or my own project?
- **Goal:** decision? plan? checklist? agenda?
- **Depth:** quick win (15 min) or deep dive (2–4h)?
- **Inputs:** what do I already have (flow, logs, brief, constraints)?

---

## 3) Still not sure?
1) Pick the closest category
2) Or ask: "which expert?" and paste 3 sentences of context
